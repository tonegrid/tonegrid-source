The previous generic artwork/content review flags were conservative inventory flags, not evidence that every listed file lacked permission. This refinement preserves the content and distinguishes the actual planned distribution.

Its per-entry and global permission statements are retained. Named existing confirmations include OutmodedElectronics, 2dor, rxguitar, gloo and Mad Hatter Amp Lab. - The pedal catalogue records creator redistribution permission confirmed by the publisher on 12 October 2026. Its nine banks contain 151 NAMs. Credits separately identify the 21 authorized MaestrodiMusica Blues Driver captures and bastienw's pack. Those existing consents are not reopened.
- The owner's 24 October confirmation covers Hiwatt pack 64577 in ToneGrid and the private GitHub content archive. The public repository will not contain that NAM payload, so no new standalone-public-model permission is presumed or requested for the present source plan.
- The amp/IR studio thumbnails commissioned or generated for ToneGrid are not automatically third-party capture-session photographs. Hiwatt's recorded image-generation recipe is one concrete provenance example. ToneGrid-authored/generated art needs a project asset licence decision, not a repeat request for the already approved Factory inclusion.
- Third-party code retains its actual licences/notices and exact source trees. Unknown historical upstream commit IDs are provenance limitations, not automatic missing redistribution permissions when the included source/notices establish the licence.

These are recorded publisher confirmations, not a new legal opinion or a grant of recipient/downstream rights. The underlying T3K restrictions and creator credits stay intact.
